E-Waste Management Report: Gujarat (2025)

Name: Onkar shelke Roll Number: 25120C2002

Introduction

Gujarat is one of India's leading industrial states and generates significant electronic waste (e-waste). Proper collection, recycling, and environmentally sound disposal help protect the environment and conserve resources.

Objectives

  • Study e-waste management in Gujarat. • Promote responsible recycling. • Encourage awareness and EPR implementation.

Current Scenario

Major cities such as Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, and Rajkot contribute significantly to e-waste generation. Authorized recyclers and collection systems support formal processing, though public participation and awareness can be further improved.

Challenges

Informal recycling, improper disposal, and limited awareness remain key challenges.

Recommendations

Expand collection centres, strengthen awareness campaigns, improve formal recycling capacity, and ensure compliance with E-Waste Management Rules.

Conclusion

Effective implementation of regulations and public participation can help Gujarat achieve sustainable e-waste management.
