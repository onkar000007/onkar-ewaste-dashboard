The Novel Idea

  • Give old electronics a second life.
  • COLLECT
  • Old phones, laptops, batteries, speakers and electronic components.
  • RECOVER
  • Test devices and recover components that still have useful life.
  • CREATE
  • Upcycle components into affordable smart and educational products.
  • Examples: Old smartphones → CCTV • Old speakers → Bluetooth speakers • Components → STEM kits

Revenue Generation

  • Multiple income streams make the model sustainable.
  • PRODUCT SALES
  • Sell refurbished and upcycled smart products.
  • E-WASTE SERVICES
  • Charge organizations for collection and e-waste management.
  • STEM KITS
  • Sell educational electronics kits to schools and colleges.
  • REFURBISHMENT
  • Repair and upgrade old devices for customers.
  • RECYCLING PARTNERS
  • Supply unusable components to authorized recyclers.
  • CORE REVENUE
  • Product sales + service fees
  • GROWTH
  • More collection → more products
