45-Minute Miro Workshop — Research-to-Roadmap Sprint (QuickPay bill-split)

This document reconstructs what the six-frame board would contain after a team actually ran the session end-to-end, following the agenda and facilitator scripts in the workshop brief. Use it as a worked example — either as a reference before running the real session, or to seed the board directly if you connect Miro.

Roles assigned

  • Facilitator — runs the timer, moves board sections
  • Timekeeper — Priya
  • Scribe — Dev (moves stickies, clusters ideas)
  • Contributors — Aisha, Rohan, Meera (3 more participants)

Frame 1 — Research Recap (0:05–0:12, 7 min)

Six pre-loaded pain-point stickies, read silently, then dot-voted (3 dots each):

  • Splitting a bill takes 6+ taps — 14 votes ⭐ WINNER
  • Users forget who owes what after settling — 6 votes
  • No way to split unevenly (e.g. one person ordered extra) — 5 votes
  • Payment requests get lost in chat threads — 4 votes
  • Users don't trust the app enough to save card details — 2 votes
  • Group members drop off before everyone pays — 1 vote

Deliverable: Agreed pain point — "Splitting a bill takes 6+ taps."

Frame 2 — How Might We (0:12–0:22, 10 min)

HMW statement: How might we let a user split a bill in one or two taps?

Crazy-8s sprint (8 min, 5 participants × 8 ideas = 40 raw ideas), clustered by the Scribe into 3 labelled groups:

  • Cluster A — One-tap contact split: auto-detect recent contacts, pre-fill an even split, single confirm tap
  • Cluster B — Smart receipt scan: camera captures the receipt, app itemizes and assigns lines to people
  • Cluster C — Voice-activated split: say "split with Alex and Sam" and the app builds the request

Deliverable: 3 labelled idea clusters — One-Tap Contact Split / Smart Receipt Scan / Voice Split

Frame 3 — Impact/Effort Matrix (0:22–0:27, 5 min)

Group shout-outs placed each cluster on the grid:

  • One-Tap Contact Split — High impact, Low effort (existing contacts API, no new hardware access)
  • Smart Receipt Scan — High impact, High effort (needs OCR + itemization logic)
  • Voice Split — Medium impact, High effort (needs voice parsing, error-prone for names)

Selected concept: One-tap contact-based split — best impact-to-effort ratio

Frame 4 & 5 — Rapid Design (0:27–0:37, 10 min)

5-step user flow

  • 1. Open QuickPay → tap "Split a bill"
  • 2. Select people from recent contacts (multi-select, checkmarks)
  • 3. Enter the total amount; app shows a live even-split preview per person
  • 4. Optional: tap "Adjust" to move to a custom/unequal split
  • 5. Tap "Send requests" → each person gets a payment request instantly

3 wireframe screens (one per pair)

  • Screen 1 — Select Contacts: search bar, recent-contacts list with checkboxes, running count "3 selected"
  • Screen 2 — Amount & Split Preview: large amount field, avatar row showing each person's share, "Adjust split" link
  • Screen 3 — Confirmation: "Requests sent!" state, per-person status chips (Paid / Pending), share button

Deliverable: 5-step flow diagram + 3 rough wireframe screens (Select Contacts → Amount & Split → Confirmation)

Frame 5 — Prototype Walkthrough (0:37–0:42, 5 min)

Volunteer (Rohan) clicked through the linked flow narrating it as a first-time user: opened the app, picked 3 contacts, entered ₹1,200, saw an even ₹400 split, sent requests, and landed on the confirmation screen with two "Pending" chips.

Flagged open questions from the group:

  • What happens if a selected contact isn't on QuickPay yet?
  • Can a user edit the split after requests have already been sent?

Deliverable: Clickable 3-screen flow + 2 flagged open questions (non-user contact handling, post-send edits)
