Sheet: Instructions

State E-Waste Management Tracker - Scoring Methodology
A 100-point scorecard for monitoring state-level e-waste generation, collection, formal treatment, compliance, and health/environmental safeguards, in line with the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 (CPCB, India).
#CategoryWeightIndicators (data source sheet)
1Generation & Inventory15District reporting completeness; category-wise breakup; annual inventory update -> '1_Generation_Inventory'
2Collection Performance20Collection rate (%); collection centers per million population; EPR collection-target achievement -> '2_Collection_Log'
3Formal Recycling & Treatment20% processed by authorized recyclers; installed capacity vs generation; material recovery rate -> '3_Recycling_Infrastructure'
4Regulatory Compliance15% producers/EPR entities compliant; inspections conducted vs planned; penalties resolved -> '4_Compliance_Tracker'
5Informal Sector Integration10% hotspots formalized/remediated; % informal workers registered/trained -> '5_Informal_Sector_Hotspots'
6Environmental & Health Safeguards10% hotspots environmentally monitored; health screening camps conducted -> '6_Env_Health_Monitoring'
7Awareness & Outreach10Awareness campaigns vs target; collection drives vs target -> '7_Awareness_Programs'
TOTAL100
How to use this workbook
- Enter data in the 7 numbered sheets (yellow cells = your input). Each sheet has one grey example row - replace/delete it.
- Set your benchmarks/targets on the '0_Scorecard' sheet (yellow cells) - e.g. collection centers per million population, EPR targets, inspection/campaign targets.
- The '0_Scorecard' sheet pulls live figures from all 7 sheets and computes the state's score out of 100 automatically. Do not overwrite formula cells.
- Score bands (customisable on Scorecard): 85-100 Excellent | 65-84 Good | 45-64 Needs Improvement | Below 45 Critical.
- Re-run this tracker every quarter/year to monitor trend - copy the Scorecard total into a tracking log for year-on-year comparison.
