Rules used, explained

EPR target (Step 2) is based on the phased recycling obligation in the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 — Producers must recycle 60% of their annual e-waste in FY2023-24 and FY2024-25, rising to 70% in FY2025-26 and FY2026-27, and 80% from FY2027-28 onward. This calculator applies that percentage directly to the "annual e-waste generated" figure entered in Step 1.

Registration fee (Step 3) comes from the fee slab CPCB uses when a Producer applies for or renews its EPR Registration, where the applicable annual recycling target (in MT) — not the raw generation figure — decides which of the five fee bands applies (₹2,500 up to ₹15,00,000).

Compliance status (Step 4) is a straightforward comparison, not a rule from the FAQ itself: if actual recycled MT is greater than or equal to the calculated target MT, the company is marked "Compliant"; otherwise "Non-Compliant". This mirrors how CPCB's quarterly/annual returns are ultimately assessed, but the pass/fail label itself is this tool's own logic layered on top of the target figure.
